Settings & Data Management

Backup and Restore

Because FarmLedger works entirely offline, your data is stored locally on this device. It's extremely important to export your data regularly to prevent data loss if you clear your browser history or change devices.

Export Data

Download all your farm data to a JSON file.

Restore Data

Import data from a previously downloaded JSON file.

Danger Zone

Permanently delete all data from this device. This cannot be undone.
